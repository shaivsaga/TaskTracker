# TaskTracker
A Notion-esque to-do list website for my classmates who struggle to keep up with schoolwork.

As class president, I want to take initiative and support everyone so that nobody falls behind.

My goal is to set a great example of responsibility and care for my own classmates. By keeping this to-do list up to date, I hope to make it easier for everyone to stay organized with their tasks while also reducing their own stress.



Features

To-do list: Due date, Status (not started, in progress, done), Comment, Notes (allow images), a “Done” overview, Visual Display of a Calendar, and data stored locally.

Personal To-do list: Same features as the public To-do list, but no comments. Your personal to-do list is private and only visible to you, so no one else can view your tasks or notes.

Settings (QOL): View your to-do list snapshot, dark mode, data export to CSV, progress charts, transfer your data, reset all data, and a chatbot on messaging platforms that messages and notifies you of your to-dos in (x) time before the due date (in which x can be set periodically in intervals). You can easily connect the chatbot to your preferred messaging app by following the simple setup instructions on the website. Once connected, the chatbot will automatically send you reminders before your tasks are due, making it easier to keep track of your assignments.

Note: I will update the to-do list regularly to reflect and correspond with our current assignments.
